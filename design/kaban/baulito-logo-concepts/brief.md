# Kaban — Baulito logo exploration

Assumption: retain the existing outlined Kaban wordmark; replace the Seed Keeper symbol with Baulito. Audience: people managing everyday money. Character: warm, watchful, dependable, distinctly Filipino. User-supplied chest reference is the character anchor.

Keep: barrel lid, oversized attentive eyes, central keyhole. Simplify: grain, bolts, weave and perspective detail; these belong in larger mascot illustrations. Avoid finance clichés: growth arrows, shields, peso signs and generic bank buildings.

Word map: kaban → chest → safekeeping; Baulito → companion → eyes; everyday money → reassurance → stable base. Intersection: a watchful little chest.

Exploration scores (clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / small-size, each out of 5):

1. **Little Keeper — mascot:** front-facing barrel chest with two generous eyes and a central keyhole (5/4/5/5/5). Selected A.
2. **Curious Chest — pictorial mascot:** side panel and raised lid give Baulito the reference's curious three-quarter stance (5/5/3/5/3). Selected B.
3. **Watchful Seal — emblem:** a barrel arch encloses two eyes above a keyhole, reducing Baulito to a compact face (4/4/5/4/5). Selected C.
4. K-shaped chest straps — letterform, but weakens facial recognition (3/4/3/3/3).
5. Keyhole as pupil — abstract mascot, but makes eyes mismatched (3/4/4/3/3).
6. Peeking chest behind wordmark — combination, but depends on lockup size (4/4/3/4/2).
7. Waving chest — mascot, but hand adds small details (5/3/2/5/2).
8. Woven chest medallion — emblem, but pattern competes with eyes (4/4/2/5/2).
9. Open chest smile — pictorial, but loses the closed-vault personality (4/3/4/3/4).
10. Two eyes inside a K — letterform, but reads as another character (2/4/4/2/3).

Construction: 256-unit square, broad filled silhouettes, 16-unit structural divisions, circular eye holes and pupils, no live text, textures or effects. A uses a symmetric dome and stable base. B uses an inset side panel and a rising top edge. C removes the box base in favour of a thick arch and open lower corners.

Black-first exploration only. The current wordmark is reused as outlined artwork in the concept lockups. Reference-library study: Discord (clear eye holes), GitHub (silhouette), Android (geometric character construction); none are traced. Library chest-subject search returned no matches.

Concepts are proposals; application assets remain unchanged until direction selection. Kit, colour development and rollout follow the logo-design skill checkpoint.
