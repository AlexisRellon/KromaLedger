# Kaban — concept round 1

Design brief: a private, local-first personal finance app named for the Filipino family treasure chest. Express keeping, ownership and everyday reassurance. Assume Filipino individuals and households are the primary audience, with app icons and mobile headers the main uses. Adjectives: grounded, personal, enduring, quietly capable. No existing palette is assumed.

Avoid: bank shields, padlocks, currency signs, growth arrows, coins and decorative heritage motifs. The cultural connection comes from the user's chest metaphor; these marks do not claim to reproduce a historical Filipino craft pattern.

Word map: kaban → chest, lid, hinge, joint, keepsake; local-first → within, boundary, possession; household → continuity, care, shared value. Intersections: a K inside a chest; joined corners surrounding value; a K opening on a hinge.

## Longlist

Scores: clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / small size, each out of 5.

1. Keepsake K — a K cut into a chest whose horizontal seam remains visible at its edges. Combination, 5/4/4/5/4. Selected A.
2. Within — opposing chest corners hold a single diamond-shaped space of value. Abstract, 4/4/5/4/5. Selected B.
3. Hinged K — separated 45-degree arms make a K that suggests a lid and its base. Letterform, 5/3/5/4/5. Selected C.
4. Family joint — two interlocking dovetails form a K. Letterform, 3/4/3/5/3.
5. Quiet latch — the two a counters in Kaban become rectangular clasps. Wordmark, 3/3/4/4/2.
6. Stored light — a chest-shaped surround encloses a four-point glint. Pictorial, 4/2/4/4/4.
7. Continuous chest — one folded ribbon returns to form its own container. Abstract, 3/3/3/4/3.
8. Heirloom seal — a compact K held by a square seal with clipped corners. Emblem, 4/2/4/4/4.
9. Homeward — two inward brackets form the sides of a lower-case k. Letterform, 3/3/4/4/4.
10. Kept name — a custom Kaban wordmark joins the b bowl to the next a. Wordmark, 3/4/3/4/2.

## Construction

A: a 208 × 176 chest silhouette with 24-unit corners on a 256 square. A 28-unit K upright is a true negative-space counter; two short side seams imply the lid without a padlock. Version 2 widens the seam from 16 to 20 units and reduces its depth to preserve the surrounding black bridges.
B: two opposing 120-unit corners, 32 units thick, enclosing a 64-unit diamond. All diagonals are 45 degrees. This is an abstract construction, not a traditional weave motif.
C: a 34-unit upright and two detached 45-degree arms, separated by a horizontal hinge gap. Version 2 reduces the upright from 40 to 34 units to match the diagonal arms' perpendicular thickness. Broad planes avoid tiny hinge hardware.

Lockups share hand-constructed Kaban letterforms. No font dependency or licensed font is embedded. Presentation labels use the concept-sheet script's system typography.

## Scope

Concept artwork only. No app integration, product rename, production icon exports or final palette. Full kit follows selection per the logo-design skill checkpoint. Local reference-library comparison is a design aid, not a trademark search.

## Review

Current overview: kaban-concepts.png. Current artwork: a-keepsake-v2.svg, b-within-v1.svg, c-hinged-v2.svg and their matching lockups.

Two rounds rendered in Chrome and visually inspected, including the 16/32/64 size ladder, black and reversed versions, mirror/rotation, context examples and the local fintech reference shelf. SVG audits report no issues for the current symbols and lockups; this is a structural heuristic, not trademark clearance. Lettering is custom path geometry with no live text.

A is recommended for its connection to the supplied chest story; its seam may also suggest a badge. B stays clear at small sizes but can suggest a scanner/focus target. C reads as K with the simplest geometry, but is less distinctive among technology lettermarks. On dark backgrounds use the white version, never the black drawing. Final colour and reversed optical compensation remain for the selected direction.
