# Kaban / Baulito usage guide

The primary signature pairs Baulito's Curious Chest (approved Option B) with Bantayog Regular. Keep the built-in 15-degree tilt, expressive eyes and central keyhole. Use the supplied outlines; preserve their proportions and spacing.

| Colour | HEX | RGB | Approximate CMYK |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chestnut — primary | #71452E | 113, 69, 46 | 0, 39, 59, 56 |
| Ivory — light surface / reverse | #F5EEDD | 245, 238, 221 | 0, 3, 10, 4 |
| Ink — dark surface | #211B17 | 33, 27, 23 | 0, 18, 30, 87 |

Chestnut on ivory: 7.00:1. Ivory on ink: 14.72:1. White on chestnut: 8.10:1. CMYK is indicative; ask the printer to proof. Pantone is unassigned and requires a physical match.

**Clear space:** keep at least 32 units on the 256-unit symbol canvas beyond every visible edge of the complete mark. Supplied viewBox padding does not replace this external clear space.

**Minimum sizes:** standard symbol 32 px / 8 mm; simplified small symbol 16–31 px; horizontal signature 160 px / 35 mm wide; stacked signature 140 px / 30 mm wide; wordmark alone 120 px / 30 mm wide. Measure pixels in the SVG viewBox footprint. Review print samples on their actual material.

**Light backgrounds:** chestnut or black. **Dark backgrounds:** ivory or white; use the supplied optically compensated reverse. **Busy backgrounds:** place the mark on a solid ivory or chestnut field. Never use chestnut directly on ink. Use the app-icon files for filled tiles and the maskable asset where a platform crops icons.

**Typography:** Bantayog Regular is the main brand/display face. Keep its rough contours; use a readable text face for paragraphs. Do not set long explanations in the logo typeface. “Kept for tomorrow.” is optional supporting copy, not part of the logo master.

**Avoid:** stretching, changing the built-in tilt, outlining, shadows, gradients, woodgrain or tiny bolts, replacing Bantayog with another font, rearranging the lockup, cropping the lid or using the full wordmark as a tiny favicon. Use the dedicated small cut below 32 px; its merged side panel and smaller pupils retain clarity.

**Choose the file:** SVG for UI, web and scalable artwork; transparent PNG for slides/documents; `web/favicon.ico` and `favicon.svg` for browser tabs; `web/apple-touch-icon.png` for Apple home-screen use; `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` and `maskable-512.png` for PWA integration. Preserve an existing app manifest's settings when adopting the supplied assets.
