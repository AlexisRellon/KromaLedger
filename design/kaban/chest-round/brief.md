## Construction and rationale

Seed Keeper: 192-unit lid; 48-unit central bridge spans the two side seams; body has 24-unit bottom corners. A pointed seed-shaped keyway replaces the usual circular keyhole. Family valuables and a seed retained for the future share one enclosed silhouette. Recommendation: the clearest relationship to the supplied chest references. Risk: may read as a conventional safe if the seed detail is lost.

Carved Seal: an octagon formed from a square with four 64-unit corner cuts. The negative keyway starts as a 45-degree diamond and continues into a straight 32-unit slot. It abstracts the references' central latch and geometric carving. Risk: the lock/security reading is stronger than the chest story.

Folded Guardian: two large wing planes join in a low, flat container. A central 32-unit channel terminates inside the solid base. The mass is protective without drawing eyes, feathers or a full bird. It is an abstract guardian, not a species-identifying Philippine eagle illustration. Risk: can read as a U or an armored badge before wings.

Prior concepts and font comparisons are retained. No app integration or full production kit in this round. Bantayog's full external EULA remains unverified.

## Refinement and validation

Current overview: kaban-bantayog-concepts.png. Current symbol files: seed-keeper-v2.svg, carved-seal-v1.svg, folded-guardian-v2.svg; matching Bantayog lockups are alongside them.

Seed Keeper v2 widens the chest to a 224-unit lid and 208-unit body, reduces the lid height to 32 units, and snaps the keyhole tail edges to 15 degrees. This brings its proportions closer to the broad reference chests. Folded Guardian v2 uses 45-degree wing edges and adds a separate central diamond head; its chamber widens to 48 units. The head makes the figure more legible but introduces an owl-like reading as well as a winged guardian. It is not a literal eagle.

Both rounds were rendered in Chrome and visually inspected. Preview sheets include the size ladder, enlarged favicon pixels, reversed artwork, mirror/rotation, contexts and a local fintech reference shelf. The current three symbols pass the structural SVG audit without issues. The builder checks that all three lockups parse and contain path-only logo artwork. The seed silhouette becomes a generic keyhole at the smallest size; do not rely on the seed metaphor being legible at 16 px. Final small-size cuts and reversed optical compensation follow selection.
